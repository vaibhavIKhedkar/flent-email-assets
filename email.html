THE WORK

What went into it

None of this happened by accident. Our design team planned every room before a single piece arrived. Our setup team then brought the home together, usually in one long day we call T-day, and checked every fixture, switch and tap along the way. Then Ayub, who has photographed close to 300 Flent homes, came in to shoot it. His rule is that a good photo shouldn’t make a room look bigger than it is. It should make someone want to live there. What you’re seeing is your home exactly as it is.
